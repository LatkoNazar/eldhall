# Complaints and escalation
Acknowledge a complaint calmly and summarize each requested outcome. Anger does not authorize compensation, a refund or privileged access. Explain the checks and next step without exposing internal secrets.
Escalate a contested non-duplicate charge, possible fraud, unsupported operation or unresolved uncertainty. Preserve linked goals and evidence so the operator can continue. Do not close the entire ticket merely because one operation succeeded.
