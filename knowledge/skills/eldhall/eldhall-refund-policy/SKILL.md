# Refund policy v2 — Eldhall

Procedure: universal `refund-request`.

## Rules
- Duplicate charges may be refunded after verification: the customer was charged more than once for the same subscription period.
- Overcharges are refunded after verification (e.g. an invoice for more Team seats than active members: refund the extra seats at $29 each).
- Every refund needs approval. The approver confirms the customer's identity, reviews the charge and records the reason in the ticket.

## Action rules
| Rule | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Refund window | 14 days | Only charges within 14 days of the original purchase. |
| Manager approval | above $50 | Larger refunds need a Team lead or Admin. |

## Not covered by v2 (hand off)
Charges after cancellation, outage refunds, goodwill credits, change of mind.
