# Cancellation policy v2 — Eldhall (draft)

Procedure: universal `cancellation`. While this is a draft, do not quote these rules: acknowledge the request and hand off.

## Rules (apply once published)
- Cancel at the end of the billing period; access stays active until then. No partial refunds for unused days.
- Before cancelling, offer a 1-month pause once.
