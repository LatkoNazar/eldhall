# Subscription plans v3 — Eldhall

Procedure: universal `billing-and-plans`.

## Plans
| Plan | Price |
|---|---|
| Starter | $9 / month |
| Pro | $29 / month |
| Team | $29 per seat / month |

## Rules
- Upgrades are prorated: they take effect immediately and the difference is charged the same day.
- Past-due accounts keep access for 7 days after a failed renewal.

## Action rules
| Rule | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Trial extension | up to 7 days | Once, only when a known bug blocked usage. |
